Football Kaise
Khelte Hain

Ek gol, gyarah khiladi, aur nabbe minute ka game. Yahan aapko soccer ball khelne ke bunyadi usool, positions, skills aur fouls sab kuch seedhe seedhe alfaaz mein samjhaya gaya hai.

Shuru Karein Match Ka Tareeqa
Bunyadi Usool

Game Shuru Karne Se Pehle Ye Teen Baatein Samjhein

Football ka maqsad simple hai — ball ko doosri team ke goal mein dalna, aur apna goal bachana. Lekin uske liye kuch bunyadi rules zaroor jaan lein.

Do Teamein, Gyarah-Gyarah Khiladi

Har team mein ek goalkeeper aur das field players hote hain. Sirf goalkeeper apne penalty box ke andar hath se ball choo sakta hai — baqi sab sirf pair, seena ya sar istemal karte hain.

Match Ka Waqt

Professional match do halves mein hota hai, har half 45 minute ka. Beech mein 15 minute ka break hota hai jise half-time kehte hain.

Goal Kaise Banta Hai

Jab poori ball goal-line paar karke goalpost ke andar chali jaaye, tab ek goal count hota hai. Jis team ke zyada goal hon, woh match jeet jaati hai.

Match Ka Tareeqa

Ek Match Kis Tarah Aage Badhta Hai

Agar aap pehli baar khel rahe hain ya dekh rahe hain, ye sequence yaad rakhein.

Kick-off Se Shuruaat

Match center circle se ek team ke kick-off se shuru hota hai. Dono halves ki shuruaat aur har goal ke baad bhi kick-off dobara hota hai.

Ball Ko Aage Le Jaana

Khiladi passing aur dribbling se ball ko opponent ke goal ki taraf le jaate hain. Ball sirf pair, sar ya seene se control ki jaati hai.

Ball Line Se Bahar Chali Jaaye

Agar ball sideline se bahar jaaye to throw-in hota hai. Agar goal-line se bahar jaaye to goal-kick ya corner-kick diya jaata hai, is baat par ke aakhri touch kis team ka tha.

Foul Ya Offside Par Whistle

Referee foul ya offside par khel roak kar free-kick deta hai. Penalty box ke andar foul hone par penalty kick di jaati hai.

Full-time Par Faisla

Dono halves khatam hone ke baad jis team ke zyada goals hon woh jeetti hai. Barabar hone par, tournament ke rules ke mutabiq extra-time ya penalty shootout ho sakta hai.

Positions

Team Mein Har Khiladi Ka Kaam

Ek aam formation (4-4-2) mein khiladi is tarah pitch par khade hote hain — defense se attack tak.

  • Goalkeeper (GK)

    Goal bachata hai, sirf apne penalty box ke andar ball ko hath se pakad sakta hai.

  • Defenders

    Apni goal-line ke qareeb rehte hain, opponent ke forwards ko rokte hain aur ball clear karte hain.

  • Midfielders

    Defense aur attack ke darmiyan link banate hain — ball wapas jeetna aur passing dono unka kaam hai.

  • Forwards / Strikers

    Opponent ke goal ke sabse qareeb khelte hain aur zyada tar goals yehi karte hain.

Skills

Char Skills Jo Har Beginner Ko Practice Karni Chahiye

Match khelne se pehle inhein akele ya dost ke saath practice karein.

Dribbling

Ball ko chhote-chhote touches se apne control mein rakhte hue aage badhna. Ball ko apne pairon se zyada door na jaane dein.

Passing

Andar wale pair (inside of the foot) se pass dena sabse zyada accurate hota hai. Pass dete waqt sar utha kar dekhein ki saathi kahan hai.

Ball Control (Trapping)

Aati hui ball ko pair, seena ya jaangh se cushion karke apne qadmon ke qareeb rok lena, taake agla move fauran ho sake.

Shooting

Ghair-support wala pair ball ke barabar rakhein, nazrein ball par, aur laces (pair ka upri hissa) se sidha shoot karein.
